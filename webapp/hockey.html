The four markets

Moneyline
Who wins. Overtime and the shootout count, so there is no draw.
60-minute result
Football-style 1X2 on regulation time only. "Draw" means it went to overtime — about 23% of games.
Puck line
A ±1.5 goal handicap. −1.5 needs a win by two or more; an overtime win is always by one, so it never covers.
Total goals
Over or under 5.5 / 6.5 across the whole game. The shootout winner is credited one goal.

Edge = our probability minus the bookmaker's after removing its margin, in percentage points. Unknown to us, known to the bookmaker: starting goalies, injuries, lineups.
