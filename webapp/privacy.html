Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

PotentialPro ("we", "us") publishes football predictions and an open, graded track record at potentialpro.pages.dev. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and your rights. We are based in Nigeria and aim to comply with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

1. Without an account

You can read every prediction and the full track record without signing in. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers. Your browser stores your light/dark theme choice locally on your device; it is never sent to us.

2. If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional and exists only so you can keep your own record of the picks you follow. When you sign in, we receive from Google:

  • your email address, and
  • your name and profile picture as provided by Google (we do not display or use them).

We then store only what you choose to add:

  • the picks you tap Track on (the match, the selection, the model's probability and the bookmaker price shown at that moment), and
  • any stake amount you enter.

We never receive your Google password, and we do not access your Gmail, contacts, calendar or any other Google data.

3. How we use it

Only to show you your own record: which of your tracked picks won or lost, and your profit or loss if you entered stakes. We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone, and we do not use it for marketing or to build profiles.

4. Where it is stored

Account and tracked-pick data are stored with Supabase (database and sign-in provider). Rules in the database make every record readable only by the account that created it. The website is served by Cloudflare and the prediction service by Render; these providers may process standard technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the service. Data may be processed outside Nigeria by these providers.

5. How long we keep it

Until you delete it. You can delete your account and every tracked pick at any time from My Record → Delete my account and data. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

6. Your rights

You may access, correct, export or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, or object to its processing, by using the My Record page or contacting us. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

7. Age

PotentialPro is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, note it in the app.

9. Contact

Questions or requests: potentialproteam@gmail.com
